In plain words

Your trip stays yours.

Publishing a route doesn't expose the parts you kept private. Joining someone else's trip doesn't hand over your own.

01

Private conversations

Trip messages are end-to-end encrypted. Only people on your trip can read them.

02

You control who comes

Only the owner approves requests. Approval never gives away edit rights.

03

You control what's public

Budgets, expenses and private chat never become part of a published trip.
